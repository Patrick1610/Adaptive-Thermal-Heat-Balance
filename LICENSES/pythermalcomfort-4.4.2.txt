ATHB numerical-core attribution
================================

The scalar clothing-surface iteration and heat-balance structure in ATHB are adapted from pythermalcomfort 4.4.2 at commit 2597e88fed10fec2f40d49759ed9b74e10ce9f89. The ATHB transfer-function structure is independently documented by the cited publication and research analysis and is checked against that pinned package.

ATHB replaces NumPy/Numba vectorization with scalar Python standard-library arithmetic, exposes only the heat-load subset needed by ATHB, enforces the project's engineering envelope, caps the clothing iteration at 150 updates, and returns immutable typed results. The upstream copyright and permission notice follow unchanged.
